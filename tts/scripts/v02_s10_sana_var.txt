O phan dau, ta thay hai backbone lon: diffusion-based va autoregressive.
O phan cuoi nay, ca hai nhanh deu co nhung cach tien hoa rieng de chay nhanh hon va mo rong tot hon.

SANA dai dien cho huong lam diffusion nhe hon.
Thay vi chi dua tren U-Net truyen thong, SANA su dung Linear Diffusion Transformer de huong den sinh anh do phan giai cao hieu qua hon.

Diem dau tien la nen sau.
Neu nhieu he thong latent diffusion nen khoang 8 lan, SANA day muc nen len 32 lan.
Dieu nay lam latent representation nho hon, nghia la so token can xu ly it hon,
chi phi attention va feed-forward cung giam theo.

Diem thu hai la linear attention.
Attention truyen thong thuong co chi phi bac hai theo so token.
Khi so token tang, chi phi tang rat nhanh.
Linear attention co lam chi phi tang gan tuyen tinh hon,
giup mo hinh xu ly anh lon nhe hon.

SANA cung nhan manh Complex Human Instruction,
tuc la xu ly cac chi dan phuc tap hon tu nguoi dung,
ket hop VLMs cho image captioning,
va cac block nhu Linear Attention cung Mix-FFN de giam chi phi nhung van giu kha nang bieu dien.

Trong khi SANA toi uu nhanh diffusion, VAR nhin lai nhanh autoregressive.
Nhung thay vi tiep tuc du doan token anh tung o mot, VAR thay doi cau hoi.

VAR khong hoi: token tiep theo la gi?
No hoi: o cap do phan giai tiep theo, toan bo anh nen tro nen chi tiet hon nhu the nao?

Dieu nay giong cach con nguoi ve:
truoc tien la bo cuc lon, roi hinh khoi, roi chi tiet.
Thay vi bi mac ket trong mot duong quet mot chieu tren mat phang hai chieu,
VAR sinh anh theo thang do phan giai.

SANA va VAR di theo hai huong khac nhau,
nhung cung tra loi mot cau hoi:
lam sao sinh anh chat luong cao nhanh hon, nhe hon, va mo rong tot hon?
