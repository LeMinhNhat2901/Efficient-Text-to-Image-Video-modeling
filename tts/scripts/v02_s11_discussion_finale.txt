Khi tat ca cac manh ghep duoc dat canh nhau,
thong diep cuoi cung cua bai giang tro nen rat ro:
van de khong chi la model nao manh hon.
Van de la ta bieu dien hinh anh nhu the nao.

Chung ta bat dau tu mot prompt.
Sau do di qua co-embedding, visual words, codebook, Transformers, diffusion, guidance, latent space,
va cac ky thuat tang toc.
Bay gio, ta co the nhin toan bo Text-to-Pixels journey nhu mot co may nhieu tang.

Thong diep dau tien cua slide Discussion la: representation la chia khoa.

Neu ta bieu dien anh bang raw pixels, mo hinh phai xu ly mot khong gian khong lo.
Neu ta bieu dien anh bang latent vectors hoac tokens,
ta co the nen thong tin, giam chi phi,
va mo duong cho Transformer hoac diffusion hoat dong hieu qua hon.

Diffusion models da chung minh hieu qua rat manh,
du duoc trien khai qua U-Net hay Transformers.
Nhung kien truc chi la mot phan cau chuyen.
Cach bieu dien du lieu quyet dinh mo hinh phai lam viec trong mot khong gian nang hay nhe.

Thong diep thu hai: phep mau khong mien phi.

Nhung ket qua thi giac an tuong den tu du lieu lon va tai nguyen tinh toan rat lon.
Slide nhac den su mo rong tu ImageNet 1.2 trieu anh den LAION 5 ty anh,
cung voi hang tram gio GPU cho training.

Training dat, nhung inference moi la chi phi lap lai lien tuc.
Moi lan nguoi dung go prompt, he thong lai phai sinh anh.
Khi so nguoi dung tang len hang trieu, inference cost tro thanh van de song con.

Vi vay, cac ky thuat nhu LatentCRF, SANA, VAR hay MarkovGen khong chi la toi uu nho.
Chung la dieu kien de text-to-image tro thanh cong nghe thuc te, nhanh va re hon.

Day cung la ly do phan tutorial khong dung o day.
Sadeep se di sau hon vao MarkovGen va CMMD,
nhung cach cau truc hoa hoac danh gia generation hieu qua hon.
Ameesh se tiep tuc voi spectral autoencoders va cac ky thuat cho efficient video generation.

Vay, Text-to-Pixels khong phai la mot phep mau don le.
No la su hoi tu cua nhieu y tuong:
bieu dien, nen, alignment, tokenization, denoising, guidance, va efficient inference.

Tu chu den vector.
Tu vector den token hoac latent.
Tu noise den cau truc.
Tu cau truc den diem anh.

Va tu diem anh, con duong do dang mo rong sang video, 3D,
va nhung the gioi tuong tac hoan chinh hon.
