Truc giac diffusion rat don gian: them noise roi hoc cach loai bo noise.
Nhung de cai dat duoc no, ta can viet qua trinh nay thanh toan hoc.

Forward diffusion la mot Markov chain.
Ta bat dau voi anh sach x_0, lay tu phan phoi du lieu.
Sau do tao x_1, x_2, cho den x_T, bang cach them Gaussian noise tung buoc.

O moi buoc, cong thuc chuyen tiep co hai phan.
Phan thu nhat, square root of one minus beta_t times x_{t-1}, lam tin hieu goc yeu di mot chut.
Phan thu hai, beta_t I, them noise moi vao anh.

Beta_t la noise schedule.
No quyet dinh luong noise duoc them vao tai moi timestep.
Nhung buoc dau thuong pha huy anh rat nhe.
Cang ve sau, noise chiem uu the hon, cho den khi cau truc anh gan nhu bien mat.

Mot cach hinh hoc, mean cua phan phoi bi keo dan ve zero, con noise ngay cang thong tri.
Neu tong luong noise du lon, x_T se gan voi Gaussian chuan N zero I.

Nhung trong training, ta khong muon phai chay hang tram buoc forward chi de tao mot anh nhieu o thoi diem t.
Day la noi toan hoc cho ta mot shortcut rat dep.

Dat alpha_t bang one minus beta_t, va alpha_bar_t la tich cua cac alpha tu buoc mot den buoc t.
Khi do, ta co the lay mau x_t truc tiep tu x_0.

Noi cach khac, thay vi di tung buoc tu x_0 den x_t, ta co the nhay thang den timestep t bang cong thuc:
x_t equals square root alpha_bar_t times x_0 plus square root one minus alpha_bar_t times epsilon.

Day la cu nhay thoi gian giup training tro nen hieu qua.

Loss function cung rat gon.
Ta dua x_t va timestep t vao mang epsilon_theta, roi yeu cau mang du doan noise epsilon da duoc dung de tao x_t.
Loss la binh phuong sai so giua noise that va noise du doan.

Pseudocode training vi vay co the doc nhu mot cong thuc lap:
lay anh, chon timestep, sinh noise, tao anh nhieu, roi cap nhat mang de du doan noise.

Sampling thi di nguoc lai.
Bat dau tu x_T lay tu N zero I, roi lap tu T ve one.
O moi buoc, model du doan noise va ta thuc hien mot reverse step de lam anh sach hon.

Den cuoi vong lap, thu tung la pure noise tro thanh mot anh co cau truc.
