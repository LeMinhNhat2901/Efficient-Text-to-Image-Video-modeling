# Prototype Storyboard

## Scene 0 - Roadmap

1. Title appears at the top.
2. Three columns appear: visual, concept, key math.
3. Ten rows fade in from top to bottom.
4. A focus box scrolls through the rows, dimming completed rows.

## Scene 1 - Forward / Backward + OU / Wiener

1. Clean data corrupts into Gaussian noise.
2. Gaussian noise is denoised back into a sample.
3. OU process shows mean reversion plus random forcing.
4. Wiener paths show continuous-time noise.
5. A bridge asks why both directions can be handled transition by transition.

## Scene 2 - Markov Chain

1. Horizontal chain appears from x_0 to x_T.
2. Markov property highlights only x_{t-1} and x_t.
3. Past states dim to show conditional dependence.
4. Joint probability factorization appears as transition pairs are boxed.

## Scene 3 - Reverse Markov Chain

1. Forward chain appears.
2. Reverse chain appears below it.
3. Bayes inversion formula appears as exact but impractical.
4. Learned Gaussian reverse conditional appears.
5. Mean is highlighted as the learned part.
